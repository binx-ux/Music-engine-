# Mic, music, and pads. One mix.

<p class="lede">Cuebox is a Windows mixer. Your mic, songs, and soundboard go out a virtual cable so Roblox, Discord, and other apps just see another microphone.</p>

No injection. No ripping Spotify. Games pick **CABLE Output**.

<a class="get" href="https://github.com/binx-ux/Music-engine-/releases/latest">Get Cuebox 1.1.1</a>

## What you get

- Real mic through WASAPI
- Headphones hear one mix, the virtual mic hears another
- Soundboard with hotkeys
- Optional voice chain (it stays off until you turn pieces on)
- MIT license. sell it if you want. if somebody asks for a copy, you still give it to them free
